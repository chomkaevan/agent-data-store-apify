# Property Risk Report: natural hazard report for any US address

One run per address gives you the whole natural-hazard picture, from official federal data:

- **Overall risk** with what drives it (for example "very high: hurricane, flood zone AE").
- **Flood:** FEMA flood zone and whether flood insurance is required for a federally backed mortgage, with base flood elevation.
- **Earthquake:** USGS seismic design category.
- **18 hazards** from FEMA's National Risk Index for the census tract: hurricane, wildfire, tornado, earthquake, riverine and coastal flooding, heat, hail, winter weather and more.
- **Disaster history:** federal disaster declarations in the county since 2000; full reports add flood insurance claims for the ZIP code.

Built for real estate investors, insurers, lenders, property managers and agents evaluating many addresses at once.

## Input
A list of US street addresses; choose a brief or full report.

## Pricing
Pay per report. Addresses that can't be found are free.

*Informational screening from public federal data; not a flood zone determination, insurance quote or engineering assessment.*
